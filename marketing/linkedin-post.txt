Click. Scroll. Screenshot. Rename. Repeat.

Now do it roughly 500 times.

That was the screenshot pile we worked through while preparing SOC 2 evidence at Heyou.

For teams collecting evidence, we built Heyou Capture: a local desktop app that turns a browser window full of tabs into organized screenshots.

The task wasn't technically difficult. It was exhausting: getting the whole page, keeping the clock visible, avoiding gaps, and putting every image in the right folder.

So we automated it—and we're open-sourcing the tool.

Pick a browser window. It visits the tabs, scrolls each page with overlap, and saves each tab into its own URL-named folder. Only PNGs in the export. Ready to zip and share.

You can watch folders and thumbnails arrive, see the collection grow, and get a final image count. Everything stays on your device.

The demo uses public VS Code pull requests, not private Heyou code.

The Windows beta is ready. Our experimental Mac version needs testers on Apple Silicon and Intel.

If you've ever been the person gathering the evidence, you know why we built this.

Try it—or help us test the Mac version:
